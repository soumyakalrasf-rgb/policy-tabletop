    Guardrails and how this tool should be used
    • Seats are roles, never named individuals. They cite only their listed sources; anything else is flagged "No source: reasoning only."
    • Seats built on one model tend to agree. Low disagreement is flagged as a warning, not treated as confidence.
    • Get consent before recording any meeting audio, and check with legal counsel how records and open-meetings laws treat transcripts and outputs from this tool.
    • Live sessions send the question, context and transcript to Claude on the viewer's own account. Do not paste confidential material you are not permitted to share that way.
